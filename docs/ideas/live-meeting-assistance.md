# Live Meeting Assistance

Status: deferred product idea

## What

Explore live in-meeting assistance beyond recording health, such as real-time notes, reminders, topic tracking, action-item prompts, or meeting guidance.

## Where It Comes From

- The architecture overview explicitly scopes the current PoC to recorded meeting processing, not fully live meeting assistance.
- Streaming transcription was discussed as a health signal, but broader live assistance is a larger product direction.

## Current Boundary

PoC implementation should focus on recording, post-meeting transcription, correction, summarization, and export.
Do not add live assistant behavior unless explicitly promoted.
