# Commercial Licensing and Entitlements

Status: deferred product/business architecture idea

## What

Design licensing, pricing, entitlement, and business-user models if `mena` moves from PoC to product.
This may include individual licenses, business licenses, offline entitlement behavior, update channels, and support boundaries.

## Where It Comes From

- The customer raised future pricing and licensing during local data and retention architecture discussion.
- The architecture overview records that commercial licensing is deferred and must not redefine ownership of user-created session data or weaken sovereignty guarantees silently.

## Current Boundary

No commercial licensing, entitlement checks, or pricing-related implementation belongs in the PoC unless explicitly promoted.
