# Privacy-Preserving UX Telemetry

Status: deferred research/product idea

## What

Capture product-quality signals such as correction counts, processing durations, fallback usage, review completion, and user satisfaction without storing meeting substance, participant identity, meeting topic, or meaningful user identity.

## Where It Comes From

- The customer wants high-quality UX insight while remaining oblivious to who the user is and what the meeting discussed.
- The architecture discussion separated telemetry from session records and canonical artifacts.
- `R-09` in the research backlog captures telemetry design as a future research task.

## Current Boundary

Telemetry is not PoC implementation scope.
Do not add telemetry events or storage until the research task is completed and the idea is explicitly promoted.
